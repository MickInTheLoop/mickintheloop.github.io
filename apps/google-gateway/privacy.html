Homelab Mail Gateway

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Operator: MickInTheLoop

Purpose and access

This is a personal Gmail integration. It accesses an account only after the owner grants Google authorisation. Each consuming project requires access approval, and mailbox research requires a separate bounded task approval.

The requested Google scope is gmail.modify, used for reading, draft management, custom user labels and stars. Google’s scope allows additional operations; the gateway does not expose sending, forwarding, archiving, Trash, Spam, deletion of received or sent messages, read-state changes or account-settings operations.

Data processed

Approved tasks may process email headers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, bodies, message and thread identifiers, draft content, labels, original MIME messages and attachments. Data is used for the task the owner requests, such as correspondence research, evidence preservation, drafting or organisation.

AI processing and sharing

Content returned to an AI assistant may be processed and retained by its configured service provider under that provider’s terms and account settings. The operator must review these settings before approving mailbox tasks. This policy does not promise that all email content remains on a local server or that every AI provider has the same retention or training settings.

The gateway does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising. It does not train general-purpose AI models. Gmail data must not be routed to a provider or account configuration that uses it to train general-purpose AI models. Transfers are limited to providing the user-requested functionality, subject to Google’s API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are kept in protected service storage outside project repositories and are not returned through the assistant’s tools. Trusted administrators can access the infrastructure.

Audit records include times, account and project identity, operations, task and approval identifiers, queries, relevant message/thread/draft identifiers, result counts, draft metadata and body hashes. Full email bodies and OAuth secrets are not intended to be duplicated in audit logs. Queries and metadata can themselves contain personal information.

Original emails and attachments can be exported to approved projects. Those projects control the storage and retention of their exports. Returned content can also be retained in assistant conversations under the provider’s settings.

Retention and removal

There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for gateway audit records or project exports. The operator manages retention explicitly. Deleting a Gmail draft does not delete exported evidence, audit records or assistant conversation history.

The owner can revoke Google access through their Google Account, disable a gateway account or project, remove a project grant, or let a task approval expire. Revocation prevents future access but does not automatically remove previously exported data or conversation records; those stores require separate review and deletion where appropriate.

This website

These static information pages are intended for GitHub Pages hosting. They contain no embedded analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, forms or external assets. The hosting provider may process visitor request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For technical questions, use the site’s GitHub issues without posting personal emails, attachments or credentials. Account support contact details are provided on the Google consent screen. Review the updated policy before approving a new use of account data.
