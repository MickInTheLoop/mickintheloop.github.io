Terms of use

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Operator: MickInTheLoop

Personal and approved use

The gateway is a personal tool operated by MickInTheLoop, not a public hosted mailbox service. Use only accounts, projects and data you are authorised to access. A project grant does not provide unlimited mailbox authorisation; investigations require a separate bounded task approval.

Review and responsibility

The owner reviews generated drafts, evidence exports and AI-assisted interpretations. The gateway does not send email: the owner must personally send a reviewed draft through Gmail. Draft deletion is supported, but deletion of received or sent messages and management of mailbox state or account settings are not exposed.

Preserve original evidence and verify important conclusions against it. Generated analysis can be incomplete or incorrect and does not replace original documents or professional advice where needed.

Availability and withdrawal

Google API behaviour, eligibility and authorisation can change. Access can expire or be revoked, and the operator may withdraw gateway access or discontinue the integration. No uninterrupted availability is promised.

Data and other terms

The privacy policy explains data handling. These terms do not replace Google’s terms, GitHub’s hosting terms or the configured AI provider’s terms.

Contact

Raise technical questions through the site’s GitHub issues. Keep private correspondence, attachments and credentials out of public issues. Account support details are also provided on the Google consent screen.
